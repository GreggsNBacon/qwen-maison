mAIson — logo & wordmark concepts

“Maison” is French for house: your AI, at home, on your own GPU. Below are five original logo marks and six ways to set the name. Colours borrow the app's existing coral (Claude Code button) and violet (Qwen Code button), joined in one gradient.

Logo marks

Each shown as a large app icon on dark, then at taskbar sizes (48 / 32 / 16 px) to check it still reads small.

1 · Roof-A

The roof is a capital A, the door a serifed I: the house literally spells “AI”. Most on-the-nose pun; clean at small sizes.

2 · Lit doorway

An arched front door with a warm AI spark glowing inside — “someone's home”. The friendliest and most premium-feeling option.

3 · Neural home

A house outline with a four-node network for windows. Clearly “AI”, a bit more technical; the network gets busy below 32 px.

4 · Keyhole

White house on the gradient, with a keyhole shaped like a lowercase i: private, nothing leaves the house. Boldest silhouette in a taskbar.

5 · Home chip

A processor with a roof and “AI” on the die — your GPU as the home. Nods to the gauges/hardware side; text is unreadable at 16 px.

On a light background

Roof-A, Lit doorway and Keyhole on a light surface (e.g. a white desktop or README). All keep their dark/gradient tile, so they hold up.

Wordmark formatting

The catch with mAIson: in most sans-serif fonts (Segoe UI included) capital I and lowercase l are the same stroke, so it can read “mAlson”. Options below either fix the I, or keep the word reading as maison and let colour reveal the AI.
