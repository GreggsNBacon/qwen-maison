“AI under a roof” — simple marks

Just the letters with a roof over them. Each variant is drawn from simple strokes (no font needed), shown as a big icon and at 48 / 32 / 16 px. Wordmark is option D — lowercase maison with the ai in the gradient.

Variants

1 · Classic

White AI, gradient roof, dark tile. The simplest reading of the idea.

2 · All gradient

Roof and letters share the gradient, so it reads as one shape. Softer, a little less contrast small.

3 · Serif I

Same as Classic but the I has caps, so it can never read as an l — also looks a bit like a little door frame.

4 · Lowercase ai

Matches wordmark D exactly: lowercase ai under the roof, with the i's dot as a warm coral light — like a lit window.

5 · Chimney

One small chimney makes it unmistakably a house roof rather than a caret/chevron.

6 · Inverse

White mark on the gradient tile. Boldest in a taskbar or Start menu.

7 · Lowercase ai, inverse

Variant 4 on the gradient tile.

8 · Bare mark

No tile — for README headers, docs and light backgrounds.

9 · Walls

The roof continues into walls, putting AI fully inside the house. Slightly less minimal.
